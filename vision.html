Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that shape our work and define our commitment to the Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao.

Our Vision

Looking Ahead

◆

A vibrant and united Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao that preserves its cultural heritage, promotes education, and contributes meaningfully to the social and economic development of Assam.

◆

We envision a future where every Limbu family in our district is empowered, connected, and proud of their identity. A future where our language, traditions, and values thrive alongside the progress of modern society.

Our Mission

What We Do

◆

Preserve Limbu Culture

  • — Promote Limbu language and Sirijanga script
  • — Organize cultural festivals and events
  • — Document oral traditions and history
◆

Promote Education

  • — Provide scholarships to deserving students
  • — Organize career guidance workshops
  • — Support literacy and skill development
◆

Foster Community Welfare

  • — Support families in times of need
  • — Organize health awareness camps
  • — Strengthen community bonds and solidarity
◆

Advocate for Our Community

  • — Represent community interests at various forums
  • — Work for recognition of Limbu rights
  • — Build bridges with other communities
Core Values

What We Believe In

◆

Unity

We believe in the power of togetherness. Our strength lies in our unity.

◆

Culture

We are committed to preserving and promoting our rich Limbu heritage.

◆

Education

We believe education is the key to empowerment and progress.

◆

Service

We serve with integrity, compassion, and dedication to our community.

Strategic Goals

Our Roadmap for 2026–2028

◆

Goal 1

Expand language workshops to reach 200+ students across the district.

◆

Goal 2

Establish a community resource center for cultural activities and events.

◆

Goal 3

Increase scholarship funding and support for higher education.
