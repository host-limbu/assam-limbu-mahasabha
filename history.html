Our History

A Journey of Service
Since 1972

Tracing the history of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha and its commitment to the Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao.

The Beginning

How It All Began

◆

In the early 1970s, a group of Limbu visionaries recognized the need for a unified organization to preserve their culture, language, and identity. The Limbu community, though small in numbers, had a rich heritage that was at risk of fading away.

With the support of community elders and leaders, the Assam Limbu Mahasabha was founded on January 15, 1972, in Diphu. The founding members included G.B. Limbu, K.B. Subba, and others who dedicated their lives to the cause.

Over the decades, the organization has grown and evolved, but its core mission remains unchanged: to serve the Limbu community and preserve its heritage for future generations.

◆
"The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was born out of the collective will of our people to preserve our identity and ensure that our children grow up with pride in their Limbu heritage."

— G.B. Limbu, Founding Member

Timeline

Key Milestones

1972

Foundation of Assam Limbu Mahasabha

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was established with a vision to unite and uplift the Limbu community in Assam.

1985

Formation of District Committee

The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee was formed to serve the growing community in the hill districts.

1995

First Cultural Festival

The first annual Limbu Cultural Festival was organized in Diphu, bringing together community members from across the region.

2005

Youth & Women's Wings Established

Specialized wings for youth and women were created to address the unique needs of these groups within the community.

2015

Limbu Language Initiative

A major initiative to promote the Limbu language and Sirijanga script was launched, including workshops for youth.

2024

Golden Jubilee & New Constitution

The committee celebrated 50 years of service and adopted a new constitution to guide its future growth.
