Our Objectives

Guiding our mission and community impact

Mission & Vision

Our Vision

A united, empowered, and culturally vibrant Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao, where every individual has the opportunity to thrive and contribute to the collective well-being.

Our Mission

To foster social harmony, preserve our cultural heritage, promote education and health, and empower community members through inclusive programs and collaborative action, ensuring sustainable development for all.

Core Objectives

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha is guided by a clear set of objectives that shape our work and priorities. Our primary goals include:

  • Cultural Preservation: Safeguarding and promoting Limbu language, traditions, festivals, and art forms for future generations.
  • Educational Advancement: Providing scholarships, mentorship, and skill-development programmes for students and youth.
  • Social Cooperation: Fostering unity, mutual respect, and collaboration among community members and with other communities.
  • Economic Empowerment: Supporting livelihood opportunities, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy.
  • Health and Well-being: Organising health camps, awareness drives, and promoting healthy lifestyles.
  • Youth Engagement: Creating platforms for young people to participate in community leadership and development.
  • Women's Participation: Ensuring equal representation and empowering women to take active roles in all spheres.
  • Organisational Development: Strengthening our internal systems, capacity building, and good governance.

Strategic Goals

To translate our objectives into tangible outcomes, we have defined the following strategic goals for the coming years:

  1. Expand Educational Access: Increase the number of scholarships and establish a community learning centre.
  2. Strengthen Cultural Identity: Organise at least four major cultural events annually and document our oral traditions.
  3. Improve Health Outcomes: Conduct regular health check-up camps and partner with local health authorities.
  4. Enhance Youth Leadership: Launch a structured youth leadership programme with mentorship from senior community members.
  5. Promote Women's Empowerment: Initiate skill-development workshops and advocacy campaigns for women's rights.
  6. Build Organisational Capacity: Train committee members in governance, financial management, and project implementation.

These goals are reviewed annually and adapted based on the evolving needs of our community and the progress we achieve.

Our Commitment

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of transparency, integrity, and accountability in all our endeavours. We believe that our objectives can only be achieved through the active participation and support of every community member.

We invite you to join us in this journey. Whether you are a student, a professional, a homemaker, or a senior citizen – there is a place for you in the Assam Limbu Mahasabha. Together, we can build a brighter future for our community.

- Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee
