Annual Cultural Festival 2026

Celebrating Limbu heritage and community spirit

Date May 15, 2026
Location Community Hall, Diphu, Karbi Anglong
Organizer Assam Limbu Mahasabha – Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee

Introduction

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, successfully organized the Annual Cultural Festival 2026 with great enthusiasm and overwhelming community participation. The event was held at the community hall in Diphu, bringing together members from across the region to celebrate the rich cultural tapestry of the Limbu community.

Festival Highlights

The festival commenced with a traditional welcome ceremony, followed by vibrant cultural performances. Artists and cultural groups presented traditional Limbu dances, folk songs, and musical performances that captivated the audience. The event provided a platform for both seasoned artists and young talents to showcase their skills, reflecting the strong intergenerational bond within the community.

Cultural Exhibitions and Activities

Beyond the stage performances, the festival featured a variety of interactive activities. A traditional food stall offered authentic Limbu cuisine, allowing attendees to savour the unique flavours of the community. A handicraft exhibition displayed intricate traditional artifacts, weaves, and handcrafted items, highlighting the artistic excellence of Limbu craftspeople. A poetry recitation session was also organized, where poets recited verses in the Limbu language, reinforcing the importance of linguistic preservation.

Honouring Community Elders

A special felicitation ceremony was held to honour community elders and contributors who have dedicated their lives to cultural preservation and social development. Their unwavering commitment and guidance have been instrumental in shaping the community's identity. The ceremony served as a reminder of the values of respect, gratitude, and collective responsibility.

Youth and Women Participation

The festival witnessed remarkable participation from the youth and women. Young volunteers played a key role in organizing the event, managing logistics, and ensuring a smooth experience for all attendees. Women's groups presented special cultural items and led workshops on traditional crafts. Their active involvement underscores the inclusive and progressive spirit of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha.

Conclusion

The Annual Cultural Festival 2026 concluded with a vote of thanks and a collective commitment to continue such initiatives in the coming years. The event was a resounding success, strengthening community ties, preserving cultural heritage, and inspiring a shared vision for a vibrant future. The Assam Limbu Mahasabha extends its heartfelt gratitude to all participants, volunteers, and supporters who made this celebration memorable.
