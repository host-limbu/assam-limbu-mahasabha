Annual Limbu Cultural Festival 2026

A grand celebration of Limbu heritage and unity

Event Details

Annual Limbu Cultural Festival 2026

Location: Diphu, Karbi Anglong

Date: 15 September 2026

The Annual Limbu Cultural Festival is the flagship event of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha, bringing together community members from across Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao to celebrate the rich heritage of the Limbu people.

The 2026 edition promises to be a vibrant showcase of traditional music, dance, food, and art. Performances by cultural troupes, storytelling sessions, and exhibitions of Limbu handicrafts will be the highlights of the day.

Program Schedule

  • 10:00 AM – Inauguration and welcome address
  • 11:00 AM – Traditional Limbu music and dance performances
  • 1:00 PM – Community feast (Limbu cuisine)
  • 3:00 PM – Cultural competitions (youth and children)
  • 5:00 PM – Closing ceremony and felicitation

Join the Celebration

All community members are invited to attend this grand celebration. Entry is free. For more details, please contact the district committee office.

Event at a Glance

  • Date: 15 September 2026
  • Time: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Location: Diphu, Karbi Anglong
  • Entry: Free for all
"This festival is a testament to the vibrant spirit of the Limbu community. It is a time to come together, celebrate our heritage, and pass it on to the next generation."

— Purna Limbu, President
