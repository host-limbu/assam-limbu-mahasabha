Data Security & Privacy

User Agreement

Your personal data, including photos and application details, is stored securely in the Mahasabha's protected database.

◆ Encrypted Data Storage
◆ Secure Firebase Database
◆ Private Cloudinary Image Hosting

1. Data Collection & Storage

When you submit a membership application through the Assam Limbu Mahasabha website, the information you provide — including your name, date of birth, contact details, address, family information, and uploaded passport photo — is collected and stored in the Mahasabha's secure Firebase Realtime Database and Cloudinary image storage.

All data is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (TLS) and stored in a protected, access-controlled environment. Your photo is hosted securely on Cloudinary, and your application data is stored in a private Firebase database that is only accessible to authorised administrators of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information is collected solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing your membership application.
  • Issuing an official membership certificate.
  • Maintaining accurate membership records for the district committee.
  • Communicating with you regarding your application status and membership-related updates.
  • Administrative and archival purposes of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha.

3. Who Has Access?

Your personal data is accessible only to:

  • Authorised members of the district committee (Dealing Assistant, President, and Approving Authority) who are responsible for processing and approving applications.
  • System administrators who maintain the website and database infrastructure.
  • You, the applicant, through the status-check portal using your unique reference number.

No third party, including advertisers or external agencies, has access to your personal data. Your information is never sold, shared, or used for any purpose other than those stated above.

4. Data Security Measures

The Mahasabha takes data security very seriously. We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our services is encrypted using TLS (Transport Layer Security).
  • Secure Database: Firebase Realtime Database employs robust security rules that restrict read and write access to authorised users only.
  • Secure Image Storage: Your photos are stored in a private Cloudinary account with restricted access and secure delivery URLs.
  • Access Control: Role-based access controls ensure that only authorised administrators can view, modify, or forward applications.
  • Audit Trails: All actions on your application — submissions, forwarding, remarks, approvals — are logged with timestamps for complete accountability.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you remain a member of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha. If you wish to have your data removed, please contact the district committee. However, some records may be retained for archival and legal compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you through the status-check portal.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal and archival obligations.
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time by contacting the committee.

7. Commitment to Privacy

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is committed to protecting the privacy and security of all members and applicants. We use modern, secure technologies to safeguard your information and ensure that it is used only for the purposes for which it was collected.

Your trust is important to us. We continuously review and update our security practices to stay ahead of evolving threats and to meet the highest standards of data protection.
