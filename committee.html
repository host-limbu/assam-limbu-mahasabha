How We Function

Committee Structure

Executive Body

The President, Working President, Vice Presidents, General Secretary, Assistant Secretaries, and Treasurer form the core executive team.

Wings & Cells

Specialized secretaries for culture, education, organizing, and publicity ensure focused attention on different areas.

Village Representatives

Each village elects a representative to ensure the committee's work reaches every corner of the district.

Want to Contribute?

The committee is always looking for dedicated individuals to serve the community. Whether you have expertise in culture, education, or social work, we welcome you.
