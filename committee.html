District Committee

Our leadership team and governance structure

Committee Overview

The District Committee is the highest decision‑making body of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha in the Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao districts. It is composed of dedicated individuals elected by the general membership to lead and represent the community.

The committee is responsible for strategic planning, resource mobilization, program implementation, and ensuring that the organization stays true to its mission. Meetings are held regularly to review progress and address emerging issues.

Sub‑Committees

To ensure focused attention on key areas, the District Committee has established several specialised sub‑committees. These groups are responsible for planning and executing activities within their domains.

  • Cultural Sub‑Committee: Organises festivals, language workshops, and cultural exchanges to preserve Limbu heritage.
  • Education Sub‑Committee: Manages scholarships, tuition support, and career guidance programmes.
  • Health Sub‑Committee: Coordinates medical camps, awareness drives, and wellness initiatives.
  • Youth Sub‑Committee: Engages young people through sports, leadership training, and volunteering opportunities.
  • Women's Sub‑Committee: Focuses on empowerment, skill development, and advocacy for women's rights.
  • Publicity & Outreach Sub‑Committee: Handles communication, media relations, and community engagement.

Each sub‑committee is led by a convenor and works in close coordination with the Executive Council to implement the committee's vision.
