How We Function

Committee Structure

Executive Body

The President, Vice President, General Secretary, Treasurer, and Joint Secretary form the core executive team that manages day-to-day operations.

Wings & Cells

Specialized wings for women, youth, and cultural activities ensure focused attention on different community segments.

Village Representatives

Each village elects a representative to ensure that the committee's work reaches every corner of the district.

Want to Contribute?

The committee is always looking for dedicated individuals to serve the community. Whether you have expertise in culture, education, or social work, we welcome you.
