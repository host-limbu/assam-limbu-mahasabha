New District Committee Members Elected

Committee members during the election process

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, has announced the newly elected committee members for the 2026–2028 term.

The election was held on 5 August 2026 at the district committee office in Diphu, with representatives from all major villages participating. The new committee brings together experienced leaders and fresh perspectives to serve the community.

Elected Members

  • President: Bal Bahadur Subba (Loksom)
  • Working President: Tilak Subba (Kondangwa)
  • Vice President: Dil Bahadur Subba (Khapung), Indra Bahadur Subba, Jibon Subba, Tek Bahadur Subba, Joya Limbu
  • General Secretary: Subash Subba (Kerung)
  • Assistant General Secretary: Suk Bahadur Subba
  • Assistant Secretary: Mon Bahadur Subba, Puja Subba
  • Treasurer: Jagat Bahadur Subba
  • Cultural Secretary: Narayan Subba (Khapung)
  • Organizing Secretary: Dhan Bahadur Subba
  • Assistant Organizing Secretary: Subash Subba
  • Education Secretary: Sher Bahadur Subba
  • Publicity Secretary: Yadav Subba, Raj Limbu, Santa Bahadur Subba

New Term, Renewed Commitment

The newly elected committee has pledged to work with renewed energy and commitment towards the welfare of the Limbu community. Key priorities include expanding educational scholarships, organizing cultural events, and strengthening community outreach programs.

The committee extends its gratitude to all members who participated in the election process and looks forward to serving the community with dedication and transparency.

Felicitation Ceremony

A formal felicitation ceremony for the new committee will be held on 25 August 2026 at the community hall in Diphu. All community members are invited to attend and extend their support to the new leadership.
