About Us

Know Our Sabha

Preserving culture, empowering community

Who We Are

Introduction

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is a community-based organization established to represent and serve the Limbu community residing in the Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts of Assam, India. We are a non-political, non-profit organization dedicated to the social, cultural, and economic upliftment of our community.

Our committee works as a unifying force, bringing together Limbu families and individuals to foster a sense of belonging, preserve our rich cultural heritage, and address the challenges faced by our community in the modern era. We believe in the power of collective action and community participation.

Our Journey

History

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was founded in the early 2000s with the vision of uniting the Limbu community in the region. Over the years, the organization has grown from a small group of dedicated individuals to a structured committee with representation across multiple villages and towns. Our history is marked by resilience, cultural pride, and a commitment to community service.

In 2016, the Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee was formally constituted to better address the specific needs of our community in these two districts. Since then, we have organized numerous cultural events, educational workshops, and social welfare programs, earning the trust and respect of the community and the administration.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a model community organization that empowers the Limbu people through cultural preservation, education, and social development, ensuring a prosperous and united community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao.

Mission

  • Preserve and promote Limbu language, culture, and traditions.
  • Provide educational and skill development opportunities.
  • Advocate for the rights and welfare of the Limbu community.
  • Foster unity and collaboration among community members.
  • Build partnerships with government and other organizations.
Our Goals

Purpose & Objectives

Purpose

The primary purpose of the Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is to serve as a representative body for the Limbu community, addressing social, cultural, and economic issues while strengthening community bonds and identity.

Key Objectives

  • To organize cultural and social events that celebrate Limbu heritage.
  • To promote education and literacy among community members.
  • To provide a platform for community voices and concerns.
  • To support members in times of need through welfare initiatives.
  • To maintain a strong and transparent governance structure.
  • To collaborate with other communities and organizations for mutual benefit.
Our Identity
