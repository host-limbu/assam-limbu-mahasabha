About Us

Preserving Limbu Heritage
in the Hills of Assam

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, has been serving the community since 1972.

Our Mission & Vision

We are dedicated to the social, cultural, and educational upliftment of the Limbu community in the Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao districts of Assam. Our work focuses on preserving our rich heritage, promoting the Limbu language and Sirijanga script, and fostering unity among Limbu families across the region.

Through cultural festivals, educational programs, and community outreach, we strive to build a stronger, more connected community that honours its past while embracing the future.

Our History

A Legacy of Service Since 1972

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was established in 1972 with the aim of uniting the Limbu community across the state. The Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee was formed shortly after to address the unique needs of the hill districts.

Over the decades, the committee has been instrumental in organising cultural events, advocating for community rights, and preserving the Limbu identity in the region.

Milestones

  • 1972 — Foundation of Assam Limbu Mahasabha
  • 1985 — First cultural festival in Diphu
  • 2000 — Language preservation workshop launched
  • 2015 — Scholarship program initiated
  • 2026 — New committee elected for 2026–2028
