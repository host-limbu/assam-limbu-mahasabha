Annual Cultural Festival 2026

A vibrant celebration of Limbu culture with music, dance, and traditional cuisine. The festival brought together community members from across Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao to showcase our rich heritage.

The event featured performances by local artists, traditional Limbu cuisine stalls, and interactive workshops on language and crafts. It was a wonderful opportunity for the younger generation to connect with their roots and for elders to pass on their knowledge.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to all the volunteers, performers, and attendees who made this event a grand success. Your support strengthens our community bonds and keeps our culture alive.

Stay tuned for more events and activities planned throughout the year.
