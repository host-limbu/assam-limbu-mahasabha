Census Audit Report

Demographic data and community statistics

Executive Summary

This census audit report provides a comprehensive overview of the Limbu community across the Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao districts. The data presented here has been collected through community surveys, government records, and direct engagement with village-level units. This report serves as a vital tool for planning and implementing community development programs.

The audit covers key demographic indicators including population distribution, age structure, gender ratio, literacy rates, and socioeconomic factors. The findings highlight both the strengths and challenges within the community, guiding the committee's strategic priorities.

Population Overview

The Limbu community in the Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao districts is estimated to have a population of approximately 25,000 individuals spread across 45 villages and urban settlements. The population has shown steady growth over the past decade, reflecting improvements in healthcare and living standards.

Literacy & Education

Literacy rates have improved significantly over the past decade, driven by government initiatives and community-led programs. However, there remains a gap between urban and rural areas, with women's literacy still lagging behind men's.

Employment & Livelihood

The Limbu community is engaged in a mix of traditional and modern occupations. Agriculture remains the primary source of livelihood, with a growing number of people employed in services, trade, and government sectors.

Key Findings & Recommendations

Key Findings

  • The Limbu community has a youthful population with 63% under the age of 30, highlighting the need for youth-focused programs.
  • Female literacy (64%) lags behind male literacy (76%), indicating a need for targeted educational initiatives for women.
  • Agriculture remains the primary livelihood (59% of employed), with limited diversification into other sectors.
  • School enrollment rates are relatively high (87%), but dropout rates increase significantly at the secondary level.
  • There is a notable migration of youth to urban centres for employment and education, affecting community cohesion.

Recommendations

  • Education: Establish a community learning centre and expand scholarship programs to reduce dropout rates.
  • Women's Empowerment: Launch skill-development workshops and literacy campaigns specifically for women.
  • Economic Diversification: Promote entrepreneurship and skill training in trades, technology, and services.
  • Youth Engagement: Create leadership and employment programs to retain young talent within the community.
  • Health: Conduct regular health camps and awareness programs addressing prevalent health issues.

Methodology

The census audit was conducted using a mixed-methods approach, combining quantitative surveys with qualitative interviews and focus group discussions. Data was collected from 45 villages across both districts, with a representative sample of 2,500 households. Government records and census data were also consulted to validate and supplement the findings.

The audit was carried out by a team of trained enumerators from the community, ensuring cultural sensitivity and accurate data collection. The findings were reviewed and validated by community elders and committee members before finalisation.
