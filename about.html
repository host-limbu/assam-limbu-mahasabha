About Us

Assam Limbu Mahasabha – Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee

Dedicated to cultural preservation, social cooperation, educational advancement, and inclusive community development.

Our Story

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, is a community organization committed to the holistic development of the Limbu community. We strive to preserve and promote our rich cultural heritage while fostering social harmony, educational growth, and economic empowerment.

Our initiatives focus on youth participation, women's empowerment, organizational capacity building, and the overall welfare of our community members. Through collaborative efforts and inclusive programs, we aim to create a stronger, more united community.

The committee was established with the vision of bringing together Limbu people across the districts to work collectively for the betterment of our community. Over the years, we have grown into a trusted institution that represents the aspirations and concerns of our people.

Mission & Vision

Our Vision

A united, empowered, and culturally vibrant Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao, where every individual has the opportunity to thrive and contribute to the collective well‑being.

Our Mission

To foster social harmony, preserve our cultural heritage, promote education and health, and empower community members through inclusive programs and collaborative action, ensuring sustainable development for all.

Our Achievements

Over the years, the Assam Limbu Mahasabha has made significant contributions to the community. Some of our key achievements include:

  • Organised over 25 cultural events celebrating Limbu heritage.
  • Awarded scholarships to more than 100 meritorious students.
  • Conducted 15 health camps serving over 2,000 community members.
  • Established a community learning centre for language and skill development.
  • Created a platform for youth leadership and women's empowerment.
  • Published a directory of Limbu community resources and services.

We are proud of these milestones and remain committed to achieving even more in the years ahead.
