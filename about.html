About the Committee

Preserving Limbu Heritage
Since 1972

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, has been serving the Limbu community for over five decades.

Our Story

A Legacy of Community Service

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha was established in 1972 with a vision to unite, preserve, and promote the rich cultural heritage of the Limbu community in the hill districts of Assam.

Over the years, the Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee has worked tirelessly to address the social, educational, and cultural needs of Limbu families across the region. From organizing cultural festivals to advocating for community rights, we remain committed to our founding principles.

Today, we connect with over 1,200 community members across 48 villages, fostering unity and preserving the Limbu identity for future generations.

Our Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

◆

Our Vision

A vibrant and united Limbu community in Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao that preserves its cultural heritage, promotes education, and contributes meaningfully to the social and economic development of Assam.

◆

Our Mission

  • — Preserve Limbu language, script, and cultural traditions.
  • — Promote educational opportunities for Limbu youth.
  • — Foster community welfare and social cohesion.
  • — Advocate for the rights and recognition of the Limbu community.
