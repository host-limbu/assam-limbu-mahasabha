About Us

Understanding our mission, values, and community impact

Who We Are

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, is a community-driven organization dedicated to the holistic development of the Limbu community. We are a collective of passionate individuals who believe in the power of unity, cultural pride, and social progress.

Our committee was established to serve as a representative body for the Limbu people in the districts of Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao. We work tirelessly to preserve our rich cultural heritage, promote education and health, and foster economic empowerment among our community members.

Our Values

We are guided by a set of core values that define our character and approach:

  • Unity: We believe that collective action is the key to overcoming challenges and achieving our goals.
  • Cultural Pride: We are committed to preserving and promoting the Limbu language, traditions, and art forms.
  • Inclusivity: We welcome every member of the community, regardless of age, gender, or background.
  • Transparency: We operate with openness and accountability in all our activities.
  • Empowerment: We strive to equip our community members with the skills and opportunities they need to thrive.

Our History

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha has a long and proud history of serving the Limbu community in the region. Over the years, we have grown from a small group of volunteers to a structured organisation with a clear vision and a dedicated leadership team.

Our journey has been marked by numerous milestones, including the establishment of cultural events, educational scholarships, health camps, and advocacy initiatives. Each step has brought us closer to our vision of a united and prosperous community.

Community Engagement

We believe that true progress comes from active community participation. That is why we regularly organise events, workshops, and meetings to engage with our members and understand their needs. Our door is always open for feedback, suggestions, and collaboration.

Whether you are a long-time member or a newcomer, there is a place for you in the Assam Limbu Mahasabha. Together, we can build a brighter future for our community and for generations to come.
