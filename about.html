About the Committee

Understanding our mission, structure, and goals

About Us

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, is a community organization committed to the holistic development of the Limbu community. We strive to preserve and promote our rich cultural heritage while fostering social harmony, educational growth, and economic empowerment.

Our initiatives focus on youth participation, women's empowerment, organizational capacity building, and the overall welfare of our community members. Through collaborative efforts and inclusive programs, we aim to create a stronger, more united community.

We believe in the power of unity and collective action. Our work is driven by the values of transparency, inclusivity, and respect for all members of the community.

Our Organization

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha is structured to ensure effective governance and community representation. Our organization operates through a democratically elected committee that works tirelessly to address the needs and aspirations of the Limbu community in the Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao districts.

We function through various sub‑committees and working groups that focus on specific areas such as culture, education, health, youth affairs, and women's development. This decentralized approach allows us to respond quickly to local needs while maintaining a unified vision for the community.

Our organizational structure is built on the principles of accountability, transparency, and participatory decision‑making. We encourage every member to contribute their skills and ideas towards the common good.

Committee

The District Committee is the apex body responsible for policy formulation, strategic planning, and overall supervision of all activities. It comprises elected office bearers and representatives from various community groups.

The committee meets regularly to review progress, address challenges, and set new goals. Members of the committee are dedicated volunteers who bring diverse experiences and perspectives to the table.

Key responsibilities include resource mobilization, program implementation, and ensuring that the voices of all community members are heard and respected.

For a full list of current office bearers, please visit our Members page.

Our Objectives

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha is guided by a clear set of objectives that shape our work and priorities. Our primary goals include:

  • Cultural Preservation: Safeguarding and promoting Limbu language, traditions, festivals, and art forms.
  • Educational Advancement: Providing scholarships, mentorship, and skill‑development programs for students and youth.
  • Social Cooperation: Fostering unity, mutual respect, and collaboration among community members and with other communities.
  • Economic Empowerment: Supporting livelihood opportunities, entrepreneurship, and financial literacy.
  • Health and Well‑being: Organizing health camps, awareness drives, and promoting healthy lifestyles.
  • Youth Engagement: Creating platforms for young people to participate in community leadership and development.
  • Women's Participation: Ensuring equal representation and empowering women to take active roles in all spheres.
  • Organizational Development: Strengthening our internal systems, capacity building, and good governance.

These objectives are not static – they evolve with the needs of our community. We remain committed to reviewing and updating our goals to ensure we remain relevant and impactful.
