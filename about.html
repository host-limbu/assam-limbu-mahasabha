About Us

Who we are, what we stand for, and how we serve the community

Our Story

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, was established with a vision to unite and empower the Limbu community across these two districts. Our journey began with a small group of dedicated individuals who recognized the need for a collective voice to address the social, cultural, and educational challenges facing our community.

Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant organization that serves as a bridge between tradition and modernity. We take pride in our rich cultural heritage and are committed to preserving it for future generations. At the same time, we embrace change and innovation to ensure our community thrives in a rapidly evolving world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster social harmony, preserve our cultural identity, promote education and health, and empower community members through inclusive programs and collaborative action. We believe that every individual has the potential to contribute to the collective well‑being, and we work tirelessly to create opportunities for all.

We are guided by the principles of transparency, accountability, and participatory decision‑making. Our doors are always open to new ideas and fresh perspectives that can help us serve the community better.

Our Core Values

  • Unity – We believe in the strength of togetherness and collective action.
  • Respect – We honour every individual and their contributions, regardless of age, gender, or background.
  • Integrity – We uphold the highest standards of honesty and ethical conduct.
  • Inclusivity – We ensure that every voice is heard and every member feels valued.
  • Innovation – We embrace change and seek creative solutions to community challenges.
  • Service – We are committed to serving our community with dedication and compassion.

Our Community Impact

Over the past decade, we have made significant strides in improving the quality of life for Limbu community members in Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao. Some of our key achievements include:

  • Organised over 25 cultural events that celebrate and preserve Limbu traditions.
  • Provided scholarships and educational support to more than 150 students.
  • Conducted health camps that served over 500 community members.
  • Facilitated skill‑development workshops for women and youth.
  • Strengthened community networks through regular meetings and outreach programmes.

We are proud of what we have accomplished, but we know there is always more to do. With your support, we will continue to build a stronger, more resilient community.
