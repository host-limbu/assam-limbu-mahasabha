About the Sabha

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is the official representative body of the Limbu community in the districts of Karbi Anglong and Dima Hasao, Assam. We are committed to preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage, language, traditions, and social welfare of the Limbu people.

Our committee works tirelessly to foster unity, provide community support, and ensure the overall development of our community members across the region.
