About Us

Strengthening the Limbu Community

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dima Hasao District Committee, is dedicated to the social, cultural and educational upliftment of the Limbu community in the hill districts of Assam.

We work to preserve our rich heritage, promote our language and script, and foster unity among Limbu families across the region.
