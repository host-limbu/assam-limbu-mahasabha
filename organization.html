Our Organization

Structure, governance, and operational framework

Organizational Overview

The Assam Limbu Mahasabha, Karbi Anglong & Dimag Hasao District Committee, is a democratic, community‑driven organization that operates with a clear governance structure. Our organization is built on the principles of transparency, accountability, and active participation of all community members.

We function through a tiered system that ensures efficient decision‑making and effective implementation of programs at the grassroots level. Every unit of our organization works in close coordination to achieve our shared vision.

Organizational Structure

Our structure is designed to balance strategic oversight with local responsiveness. The key components are:

  • District Committee: The apex body responsible for overall policy, planning, and supervision. It includes elected office bearers and representatives from various community groups.
  • Executive Council: A smaller group that handles day‑to‑day operations and implements the decisions of the District Committee.
  • Sub‑Committees: Specialised groups focusing on culture, education, health, youth, women, and public relations. Each sub‑committee has its own convenor and members.
  • Village/Unit Level Committees: Local bodies that represent specific communities within the district, ensuring that grassroots voices are heard.

This structure allows us to address diverse needs while maintaining a unified organisational identity. Regular meetings and reporting mechanisms ensure transparency and accountability at every level.

Geographic Coverage

Our committee serves the Limbu community across the entire Karbi Anglong and Dimag Hasao districts. We work closely with village‑level units to ensure that our programs reach every corner of the region.

By maintaining a strong presence in both urban and rural areas, we are able to understand and respond to the unique challenges faced by different segments of our community. Our geographic coverage is a testament to our commitment to inclusive development.

Membership & Governance

Membership to the Assam Limbu Mahasabha is open to all individuals who identify with the Limbu community and support our objectives. We encourage active participation from men, women, and youth alike.

Governance is conducted through a democratic process:

  • General Body Meetings: Held annually, where all members can voice their concerns and vote on key decisions.
  • Elections: Office bearers are elected through a fair and transparent process every two years.
  • Advisory Panels: Eminent community members and experts are invited to provide guidance on strategic matters.

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of ethical conduct and governance in all our activities.

Our Sub‑Committees

To effectively address the varied needs of our community, we have established several sub‑committees, each focused on a specific area:

  • Cultural Sub‑Committee: Organises festivals, language workshops, and cultural exchanges.
  • Education Sub‑Committee: Manages scholarships, tuition support, and career guidance.
  • Health Sub‑Committee: Coordinates medical camps, awareness drives, and wellness programmes.
  • Youth Sub‑Committee: Engages young people through sports, leadership training, and volunteering.
  • Women's Sub‑Committee: Focuses on empowerment, skill development, and advocacy.
  • Publicity & Outreach Sub‑Committee: Handles communication, media relations, and community engagement.

These sub‑committees are the engine of our organization, translating our objectives into tangible actions. They are led by dedicated volunteers who bring passion and expertise to their roles.
