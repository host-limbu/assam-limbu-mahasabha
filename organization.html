How I Work

From concept to completion — my process, services, and approach

My Approach

I'm Yadav Subba, an AI Assisted Web Developer & Producer. I believe in building digital solutions that are not just functional, but also meaningful, scalable, and user‑focused. Whether it's a web application, an AI‑powered tool, or a complete digital product, my process is built on collaboration, transparency, and craftsmanship.

I work with clients worldwide — from startups and small businesses to community organisations and creators. My goal is to transform ideas into reality, using modern technologies like Firebase, Cloudinary, and AI to deliver high‑quality, production‑ready results.

My Workflow

I follow a structured yet flexible workflow that ensures every project is delivered on time and exceeds expectations. The key stages are:

  • Discovery & Planning: I start by understanding your vision, goals, and requirements. This stage involves research, brainstorming, and creating a clear roadmap.
  • Design & Prototyping: I create wireframes and prototypes to visualise the user experience and gather early feedback.
  • Development & Integration: I build the core functionality using modern technologies (Firebase, Cloudinary, AI APIs, etc.), ensuring clean, maintainable code.
  • Testing & Quality Assurance: I rigorously test every feature to ensure performance, security, and a flawless user experience.
  • Deployment & Maintenance: I deploy the project and provide ongoing support, updates, and optimisations as needed.

This approach allows me to adapt to your changing needs while maintaining a clear focus on the end goal.

Global Reach

I'm based in Karbi Anglong, Assam, India, but I work with clients from around the world. My projects have reached over 20 countries, serving diverse industries and communities.

Whether you're a local startup, an international agency, or a non‑profit organisation, I'm committed to delivering the same level of dedication, quality, and professionalism regardless of location.

My Process & Communication

I believe that great results come from great communication. I keep you involved at every stage of the project.

  • Regular Updates: I provide frequent progress reports, demos, and status meetings so you always know where we stand.
  • Collaborative Feedback: Your input is invaluable. I welcome feedback and iterate quickly to ensure the final product matches your vision.
  • Transparent Process: I use tools like project boards, shared documents, and version control to keep everything organised and accessible.
  • Post‑Launch Support: I offer ongoing maintenance, bug fixes, and feature enhancements to keep your project running smoothly.

This collaborative approach has helped me build long‑lasting relationships with clients and deliver projects that truly make a difference.

Services I Offer

I specialise in a wide range of services to help you build, grow, and scale your digital presence. Here's what I can do for you:

  • Custom Web Development: Full‑stack applications, dynamic websites, and tailored digital experiences.
  • AI Integration: AI‑assisted tools, automation workflows, and intelligent features to streamline your processes.
  • Digital Production: End‑to‑end product development, from concept to deployment, with a focus on quality and scalability.

  • PDF & Certificate Generation: Dynamic PDF creation, printable certificates, and QR verification.
